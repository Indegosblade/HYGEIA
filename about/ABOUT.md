# About HYGEIA

HYGEIA is named after the Greek goddess of health and cleanliness. It prepares
copies of filesystem datasets for review through explicit sanitization rules and
reports which checks completed, failed, or remained outside scope.

The project favors understandable processing, reproducible synthetic tests, and
visible failure states. Schema-aware handling and generic patterns are followed
by verification of supported content. A passing scan is evidence within that
policy, not a claim that arbitrary data is anonymous.

Start with the [README](../README.md), [architecture](../docs/ARCHITECTURE.md), and
[verification contract](../docs/VERIFICATION.md). Historical changes remain in
the [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md).
